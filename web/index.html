Why I Built BiteSize for Mayank

Built for a friend struggling with ADHD paralysis & executive dysfunction

Mayank is my closest friend and roommate. He is brilliant, creative, and can build complex software architectures in a flow state. But on Sunday evenings, simple life tasks can trigger paralyzing overwhelm.

When an ADHD brain looks at "clean my messy room, finish OS lab, wash dishes, reply to email", it doesn't see four tasks—it perceives an insurmountable 400-pound boulder. The cognitive friction causes executive dysfunction freeze, where he gets stuck on his bed staring at his phone, trapped in guilt.

Every commercial to-do app (Notion, Todoist, Apple Reminders) made it worse by showing 50 red overdue badges, amplifying guilt and dread.

"I don't need another list telling me how behind I am. I just need someone to tell me where to put my first foot." — Mayank

BiteSize does exactly that: it ingests the entire chaotic panic brain-dump, runs open-source AI locally to shred the boulder into microscopic 2-minute atomic steps, and then hides everything except the single next step. No overwhelm. No guilt. Just pure momentum.

Mayank's Real Reaction:

"Bro, usually looking at my to-do list makes me want to close my laptop and sleep. Seeing just 'Put 3 shirts in the basket' with a 2-minute countdown actually made me get off the bed. This is the first time an app didn't make me feel guilty."
